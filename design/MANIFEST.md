# design/: manifest

The icons and graphics of the application, with their sources.

Installation: the application's `app/app.pro` installs the launcher icons and `export/ui`; the packaging installs `export/notify` and checks the whole set. The launcher icon is the second design, provisional until the phone review is done. Licence of the drawings: GPL-3.0-or-later, as the application.

Notes:
- The app SVGs use plain shapes, gradients and paths only (no text, filters or masks), as Qt SVG Tiny requires.
- Text in the drawings is converted to paths (outlines of DejaVu Sans Bold, free licence).
- The glyph colours are the provisional band fills The neutral icons use a slate disc `#566070`, a light ring `#E4E8ED` and light marks `#F6F7F9`.
- The reading box on the cover and the tile keeps its band colour; only its band cell takes the glyph.
- The other state pictograms are Silica icons (guide 2.3) and are not in this folder; the cover action Start is Silica's `icon-cover-play`.
- `build.py` (python3, Inkscape for PNG) generates the files marked "generated" and the new own pictograms; the older masters were made by throw-away scripts that are not in the repository and stay as they are. Not yet here: `palette.json`, a lint, a Qt SVG load test (no `QtSvg` on the strategy PC). All SVGs in `src/` and `export/ui/` carry SPDX lines (GPL-3.0-or-later, copyright Michele Brami 2026).
